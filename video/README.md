# Explainer video: "The Evidence Is in the Votes"

`evidence-in-the-votes.mp4`: 2:42, 1920×1080, H.264 + AAC (web-ready, `faststart`). `poster.jpg` is the
title frame; `captions.vtt` holds English captions timed to the narration.

Embed on a website:

```html
<video controls preload="metadata" poster="poster.jpg" width="1280">
  <source src="evidence-in-the-votes.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  <track kind="captions" src="captions.vtt" srclang="en" label="English" default>
</video>
```

Rebuild (`src/`): `script.json` (narration) → `tts.py` (Piper TTS, voice `en_US-ryan-high`, downloaded
separately from huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices) → `timeline.json` + `narration.wav`; `music.py`
(original procedural music); then mix, and `node render.js video` renders `anim.html` frame by frame
through Playwright and ffmpeg.

Credits: narration voice "ryan" from Piper (dataset licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, credited in the end card);
the music is generated by `music.py` for this video; the fonts are Fredoka and Nunito (SIL Open Font
License). All numbers come from the paper (`docs/submission/`).
